CHUVI V1: System Report

Back end as it stands on 14 October 2026 (code version 8ad7cc7, live on the server). Prepared as the starting point for V2.

How to read this. Every feature has the same six parts: what it does, who uses it, where it is set, the exact rule or formula, one worked example, and its status.

Live built and running now. In progress partly built, or waiting on a decision. Planned agreed but not built.

Money is written as N. Times are Lagos time. Figures are the values set on the live system today; where a value is only a default, it says so. The screens are built by the app team in a separate project; this report describes what the system does and allows.

Part 2 compares the system with the revised V1 Test Plan (back end, 14 Oct) in three lists: what already matches, what is new work, and what is built but different. It is based on reading the code; the scenarios have not yet been run end to end.

A. Accounts and staff

A1. Customer sign-up (email or Google)

Live
What it does
Creates a customer account by email and password, or with Google. Google users give their phone number on a second step.
Who uses it
Customers.
Where it is set
Fixed rules in the system.
Rule
One account per email. Phone numbers are stored in one standard form (0803…, +234803… and 234803… are the same number). Ticking the Terms box is recorded with the date and time (termsAccepted), not required. Signing up starts the First Experience offer clock and the "registered, not booked" messages (H3).
Example
Amaka signs up with Google, types 0803 123 4567 and ticks Terms. Her record shows phone +2348031234567, terms accepted 14 Oct 18:02, and her Welcome SMS goes out at once.
Status
Live.

A2. Referral code at sign-up

Live
What it does
Links a new customer to the person (or rep) whose code or link they used.
Who uses it
Customers and reps.
Where it is set
Fixed rules. Reward amounts are in Rewards settings (C5).
Rule
A code can be given at sign-up, or later in Refer and Earn, but only before the customer's first order. An unknown code, or the customer's own code, is refused with a message.
Example
Amaka opens the rep's link (…?ref=REP12). The code box is filled in. She can still add a code tomorrow; after her first order the box is hidden.
Status
Live. Gap: no admin report per rep of who signed up, who paid and how much (T1.01).

B. Booking and pricing

B1. Item price

Live
What it does
Works out the price of every piece from the price list, the service and the care level.
Who uses it
Customers (app and assistant), S1 at the counter, Change Items.
Where it is set
Admin, Items (price per item); Admin, Order settings (service multipliers and care level multipliers).
Rule
Price per piece = (item price × service multiplier, rounded to the nearest N100) × care multiplier. Live service multipliers: Wash and Iron 1.0, Wash Only 0.7, Iron Only 0.6. Care: Classic 1.0, Premium 1.5, VIP 2.0. Care can differ per item.
Example
Shirt N600. Wash and Iron: 600. Wash Only: 600 × 0.7 = 420, rounded to 400. Iron Only: 600 × 0.6 = 360, rounded to 400. Premium Wash and Iron: 600 × 1.5 = 900.
Status
Live.

B2. The bill and its lines

Live
What it does
Builds one total from separate lines, so the rows always add up.
Who uses it
Customers, S1, riders, admin.
Where it is set
Fees in Admin, Order settings; offers in the Offer Builder.
Rule
Total = items + speed charge + pickup fee + delivery fee − offer lines. Each offer, waiver and fee is its own line. Live fees: pickup N1,000, delivery N1,000 (window price). Speed charges: Express N0, Ready Today N0 (both set to zero today).
Example
10 shirts Wash and Iron, Regular, window pickup: 6,000 + 0 + 1,000 + 1,000 = 8,000. On a first order the First Experience offer takes off pickup (−1,000) and delivery (−1,000): total N6,000.
Status
Live.

B3. Delivery speeds and the ready date

Live
What it does
Gives each speed a real ready date from the pickup day and time the customer picks.
Who uses it
Customers, the assistant, S1, stations (queue order).
Where it is set
Admin, Working days and Half days; Admin, Display names (Regular, Express, Ready Today).
Rule
Count working days from the day after pickup: Tuesday to Saturday 1, Sunday ½, Monday 0. Regular needs 2, Express 1. The order is ready by 7:00 PM on the day the count is reached. Ready Today counts the pickup day itself, and is offered only for a pickup before 12 noon and never on Sunday.
Example
Regular picked up Saturday evening: Sunday ½, Monday 0, Tuesday 1½, Wednesday 2½. Ready Wednesday 7 PM. Express picked up Sunday morning: Monday 0, Tuesday 1. Ready Tuesday 7 PM.
Status
Live. Gap: Ready Today is not blocked for Quick Booking (T3.01).

B4. Daily floor limit

Live
What it does
Stops the floor being promised more pieces in a day than it can wash.
Who uses it
Every booking channel (app, assistant, S1, plans).
Where it is set
Admin, Order settings, floor limits: 150 a day, Ready Today 30, Express 50.
Rule
Pieces count on their ready day. Ready Today and Express come out of the shared 150; Regular uses the rest. Sunday gets half (75), Monday 0. A Regular order that does not fit spreads over the next working days and shows the last day. Ready Today and Express are never spread: if the share is used up they show "Full today" (Express names the day); if the order is too big: "Too many pieces for Express. Choose Standard, or call us on +234 707 325 4943." Above 200 pieces a booking is refused with the call-us message.
Example
Tuesday has 40 pieces of room left. A 180-piece Regular order puts 40 on Tuesday and 140 on Wednesday: ready Wednesday. A 60-piece Express order is too big for Express (50): Express is greyed out.
Status
Live. A booked order keeps its date when the count changes (client ruling 12 Oct). The revised test plan now asks for a new date when the real count is more than twice the booking (R21); see Part 2.

B5. Pickup and delivery windows

Live
What it does
Lets the customer choose a time window for pickup and delivery, with a limit per window.
Who uses it
Customers, the assistant, S1, riders.
Where it is set
Admin, Booking windows (name, start, end, days, cutoff, limit; blank limit = no limit). Admin, Working days.
Rule
Live window: Evening 3:00 to 6:30 PM, Tuesday to Sunday, booking closes 60 minutes before it starts. One window holds pickups and deliveries together, one order counts as one stop. A full window drops off the list, and the move is recorded for the daily "windows filled / customers moved" count. A day not ticked in Working days has no windows. The delivery window is confirmed when the order is ready.
Example
At 1:30 PM on Thursday, today's Evening window is offered. At 2:10 PM it has closed for booking, so Friday Evening is the first window shown.
Status
Live.

B6. Urgent (Anytime) pickup and delivery

Live
What it does
Sends a rider as soon as possible instead of in a window.
Who uses it
Customers; S1 is alerted and the job goes to the top of the pickup list.
Where it is set
Admin, Order settings: Urgent hours 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM; Urgent pickup fee N1,000, delivery fee N1,000.
Rule
Open on working days within the hours; outside them the app says when Urgent next opens. Free pickup and delivery offers apply to window bookings only, except when every window that day is full during Urgent hours: then Urgent is charged at the window price and the offer is kept. Refund: if the customer booked Urgent while that day's window could still be booked, and the job was done inside that window, the price difference goes back to the wallet automatically.
Example
Booked Urgent at 11:00, picked up at 4:00 PM inside the Evening window: the difference is refunded. Booked at 2:30 PM (window already closed for booking): no refund. Note: today the Urgent fee and the window fee are both N1,000, so there is no difference to refund and Urgent costs no more than a window.
Status
Live.

B7. Quick Booking

Live
What it does
Lets a customer book with a rough piece count and pay nothing. We count, bill and send a payment link.
Who uses it
Customers (app and assistant), rider, S1.
Where it is set
Fixed rules; prices and offers as B1, B2 and C1.
Rule
Nothing is paid at booking. The rider enters his count; S1 enters the real items and the system makes the bill (staff can never type an amount): items + pickup + delivery − the best offer (the one worth more on this bill; First Experience always wins on a first order). The order then waits in Payment holds with a Paystack link (D5), and goes to tagging by itself once paid. 1 to 200 pieces.
Example
Emeka books "about 5". S1 lists 4 shirts (2,400) and 2 trousers (1,400). Bill: 3,800 + 2,000 trips = N5,800 (Always Free needs N8,000, so it does not apply). Link sent; he pays; the order moves to tagging.
Status
Live. Gaps: no pay-by time, no expired-link note, no ready-date recompute (R21).

B8. Counter booking and walk-ins

Live
What it does
Lets S1 book an order at the office, for a walk-in or a registered customer.
Who uses it
S1.
Where it is set
Same prices and limits as app booking.
Rule
A payment method is required: cash, POS, bank transfer or wallet (cash is pre-selected on the screen). A wallet that is short can be topped up by another method in the same payment. Reward credit is used only if the customer says yes. Floor limits apply.
Example
Ifeoma, 3 pieces Ready Today at 9 AM, pays by POS. Order created, Ready Today share goes from 30 to 27.
Status
Live. Gap: the revised plan says no cash, and a POS receipt number (R16).

B9. Display names

Live
What it does
Renames speeds, service types and care levels on every screen without touching stored orders.
Who uses it
Admin.
Where it is set
Admin, Display names.
Rule
Only the label changes. A service type that orders depend on cannot be renamed or removed in general settings, because pricing matches on it.
Example
"same-day" is shown as "Ready Today" to customers and staff; old orders keep working.
Status
Live.

C. Offers and rewards

C1. Offer system

Live
What it does
Admin builds offers once; the system gives and applies them by rule.
Who uses it
Admin (Offer Builder); customers (My Rewards, checkout).
Where it is set
Admin, Offers: type, triggers, benefits, minimum order, customer window days, expiry, stacking flag.
Rule
Types: personal (given to one customer by a trigger such as first experience, second order, every 5th order, reactivation, recovery, referral level), promotional (open to everyone by rule) and baseline. At most one personal offer per order. A promotion does not combine with a personal offer unless that offer allows it. Every offer is re-checked at the moment it is applied, and the bill says why one was refused. A rule set to 0 means no rule.
Example
A promotion "10% off above N10,000" and a personal "free delivery" on the same order: only the personal one applies unless the promotion is marked stackable.
Status
Live.

C2. Live offers today

Live
What it does
The offers switched on now.
Who uses it
Customers.
Where it is set
Admin, Offers.
Rule
First Experience Offer (personal): free pickup and free delivery on the first order; clock starts at registration. Always Free at N8,000 (promotional): free pickup and delivery on a window booking of N8,000 or more.
Example
A first order of 10 shirts (N6,000 items): pickup and delivery free. A repeat order of N8,400 items in a window: pickup and delivery free; the same order as Urgent: trips charged.
Status
Live. To clean up before launch: First Experience has two minimums set (N4,000 on its benefits, N5,000 on the offer itself, so N5,000 is what applies); a test offer named "Drffg" is active and gives free pickup to everyone above N5,000.

C3. Reward credit (inside the wallet)

Live
What it does
Holds non-cash credit next to the wallet's cash, each with its own expiry.
Who uses it
Customers; admin can adjust credit.
Where it is set
Rewards settings, credit lifetimes: laundry 90 days, referral 45, recovery 90, promotional 30.
Rule
Credit is spent oldest expiry first, and only when the customer chooses to use it. It is taken for good only when the order completes; a cancelled order gives it back with its original expiry. Every credit row carries a reason line.
Example
Wallet cash N2,000, referral credit N400 (expires 20 Nov), laundry credit N1,000 (expires 2 Jan). A N3,000 order with credit switched on: 400 referral, then 1,000 laundry, then 1,600 cash.
Status
Live. The revised plan wants credit on by default at Pay (T2.14); today the customer opts in.

C4. Quick Booking offer pick

Live
What it does
Chooses the offer for a Quick Booking after counting, since nobody chose one at booking.
Who uses it
System, at S1's count.
Where it is set
Fixed rule.
Rule
The offer worth more on this bill (discount plus trips waived; a future credit does not count) is applied; the other stays for next time. First Experience always wins on a first order. An offer valid at booking is honoured even if it expires before counting; the minimum is checked on the counted bill.
Example
Customer holds a N500 discount and a free-trips offer on a N6,000 bill: free trips (N2,000) is worth more and is applied.
Status
Live.

C5. Referral reward and levels

Live
What it does
Rewards a customer when a friend they referred completes a first order.
Who uses it
Customers (Refer and Earn).
Where it is set
Rewards settings: reward %, maximum per reward (off), monthly cap (off), welcome amount (N0, off), levels.
Rule
Reward = level % of the friend's first delivered order, paid as referral credit (45 days), once per friend; taken back if that order is fully refunded. Levels are earned by lifetime successful referrals and never lost: Member 5%; Promoter (3) 7%; Ambassador (8) 10%; Champion (15) 15%. A monthly free-laundry credit (N2,000 / N5,000 / N10,000) is given in months the monthly target (2 / 3 / 5) is met.
Example
A Member's friend's first order is N8,000 and is delivered: N400 referral credit, expiring in 45 days.
Status
Live.

D5. Payment hold

Live
What it does
Parks an order that owes money until it is paid; nothing is tagged before then.
Who uses it
System, S1, admin.
Where it is set

Card payment clears it; S1 can record a POS or transfer payment; admin can waive it with a reason (the order is processed but cannot be dispatched until paid). Since 14 Oct, S1 cannot "release" an unpaid payment hold.
Example
Bill sent Monday 9:00. Reminders 15:00 Monday and 9:00 Tuesday; admin alert 9:00 Wednesday.

E. Order flow: riders, intake, stations

E1. Rider assignment and pickup

Live
What it does
S1 assigns a rider; the rider starts the pickup, collects and records his count.
Who uses it
S1, riders.
Where it is set
Admin, Order settings: unassigned job alert after 30 minutes, escalation after 60.
Rule
The job shows only on the assigned rider's list (Urgent first). If the rider's count differs from the booking he gives a reason; the order is flagged (not stopped), the customer gets an SMS with the count, and S1 and admin are told.
Example
Booked 10, rider counts 11, reason "customer added one". Flagged; SMS "we collected 11 item(s) … (you booked 10)".
Status
Live. Gaps: the rider must enter the customer's phone number to start and finish (plan: no check); SMS wording differs; "Customer not available" does not return the job to Needs rider or count failures (R22).

E2. Intake count and the count dispute hold

Live
What it does
S1 counts what arrived; a disagreement with the rider stops the order for admin.
Who uses it
S1, admin.
Where it is set
Hold type "Count differs from rider count" (admin only).
Rule
Same count: the order moves on. Different count: hold, admin alerted; only admin can release it. Anyone else gets "Only admin can release this." (fixed 14 Oct).
Example
Rider 5, S1 4: hold raised, admin alerted; S1's Release is refused with the sentence above.
Status
Live. Gap: no separate "Possible missing item" alert when S1 counts fewer (R22).

E3. Change Items (recount)

Live
What it does
Replaces an order's items after counting and settles the difference.
Who uses it
S1 (before tagging), admin (any time).
Where it is set
Fixed rules; prices and offers as B1 and C1.
Rule
The bill is recalculated through the same pricing and offers. Up: the balance becomes a payment hold. Down: the difference goes to the wallet automatically. A reason is required; the customer gets the new bill. After tagging starts, only admin.
Example
Paid N6,000 for 10 shirts; S1 counts 11: N600 payment hold. Counts 9: N600 to the wallet.
Status
Live. Gaps: allowed before pickup; no recount cover (10% and N200) or mismatch record (R17); plan orders are priced as money (R18); old and new items not saved.

E5. Station handoffs

Live
What it does
Moves pieces between S1, S2, S3, S4 and QC with a send and a receive.
Who uses it
All stations.
Where it is set
Fixed rules.
Rule

F. Holds and cancellations

F1. Holds and hold types

Live
What it does
Stops work on a piece or an order until a problem is solved.
Who uses it
All stations, admin (Holds Management).
Where it is set
Admin, Hold types (name, which stations may raise it, time limit, alert admin, admin only).
Rule
At S1 and for payment a hold stops the whole order; at S2, S3, S4 and QC it stops only that piece. A hold always has a reason. Time limit: the type's own limit, or else by speed (Ready Today 2 h, Express 4 h, Regular 6 h, editable); payment holds 48 h. Each hold is Active or Overdue, never both; the first time it goes Overdue admin gets one alert. Admin-only types can be released only by admin.
Example
A Regular order held at 9:00 is Active at 14:00 and Overdue at 15:30.
Status
Live. Gap: payment holds turn Overdue at 48 h; the plan says never (B33).

F2. Cancellation

Live
What it does
Cancels an order and unwinds money, credit and offers.
Who uses it
Customers, Customer Experience, S1, admin.
Where it is set
Admin, Order settings: free cancel window 15 minutes; cancellation pickup fee N1,000, return fee N1,000.
Rule
Before pickup starts, or within 15 minutes of booking: the customer cancels free, all money back to the wallet. After pickup, before tagging: the customer asks and Customer Experience decides; unpaid orders pay N1,000 + N1,000 (even with a free-trip offer); paid orders get the laundry fee back and the trips are kept. After tagging: no customer cancel. S1 can cancel before processing; admin any time, always with a reason. Credit and offers used are given back.
Example
Quick Booking picked up, bill unpaid, admin cancels: N2,000 is charged for the two trips.
Status
Live. Gaps: the plan says only admin cancels and S1 escalates; the N2,000 should go as a payment link, and the return waits until it is paid (R12, R21).

G4. In-app assistant

Live
What it does
Answers questions and takes approved actions in chat.
Who uses it
Customers.
Where it is set
Server settings (AI provider and model). Answers come from the same data and rules as the app.
Rule
The AI only works out what the customer wants; every answer and action is computed by the system, never invented. It can: give order status, wallet, offers, referral, turnaround and service facts; book (including Quick Booking, speeds with real ready dates and prices); pay from wallet or plan, or send a card link; open a complaint; take feedback; change address; change phone with a code. Each action needs a Yes first. It can never refund, approve compensation, edit credit or close a complaint. Without the AI it falls back to keyword answers.
Example
"Book the usual for Thursday": it fills in the last order, shows the speeds with ready dates, asks "Confirm?", then books.
Status
Live. Gaps: it quotes a price when asked "how much" (plan: no estimate, point to Book); it offers payment in chat (plan: none).

H. Messages and CRM

H1. Customer stages and tags (CRM)

Live
What it does
Keeps one card per customer or lead, with a stage and automatic tags.
Who uses it
S1 (intake level), admin.
Where it is set
